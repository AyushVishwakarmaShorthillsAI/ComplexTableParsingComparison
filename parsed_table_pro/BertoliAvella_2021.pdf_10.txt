### 4. Table Data
This section of the table details the genetic and phenotypic information for three patients (27, 28, and 29) with mutations in the ZNF699 gene.

Patients 27 and 28 both have the same homozygous (Hom) frameshift mutation in the ZNF699 gene, identified as c.1623_1626delTTAT at the cDNA level (reference sequence NM_198535.2), which results in a protein change denoted as p.Tyr542fs. Both patients are from families with consanguinity. Patient 27 presented with a wide mouth, facial abnormalities, a smooth philtrum, micrognathia, an abnormal electroretinogram, long eyelashes, nystagmus, syndactyly, intellectual disability, hepatosplenomegaly, and failure to thrive. Patient 28 exhibited cryptorchidism, retrognathia, a triangular face, macrotia, a prominent nasal bridge, syndactyly, intellectual disability, global developmental delay, and an abnormal facial shape.

Patient 29 has a different homozygous (Hom) frameshift mutation in the ZNF699 gene. The mutation is a duplication, c.1324dupA (reference sequence NM_198535.2), leading to a predicted protein change of p.Ser442fs. This patient is also from a consanguineous family. The associated phenotype includes microcephaly, intellectual disability, global developmental delay, hiatus hernia, developmental regression, recurrent infections, and immunodeficiency.
