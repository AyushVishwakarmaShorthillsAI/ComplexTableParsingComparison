### 4. Table Data
This section of the table presents data on four patients with homozygous mutations in the ZNF699 gene.

Patients 34 and 35 are siblings from a consanguineous family who both carry the homozygous ZNF699 mutation NM_198535.2:c.436_439del, which results in the protein change p.Asp146Ilefs*10. Patient 34, the index case, presents with hypopigmentation of hair, intellectual disability, failure to thrive, anemia, abnormal facial shape, and congenital hypoplastic anemia. Patient 35 is described as being "similarly affected as index," indicating a comparable set of clinical features.

Patient 36, from a consanguineous family, has a different homozygous mutation in the ZNF699 gene, identified as NM_198535.2:c.51_54delCTCA, leading to the protein change p.Asp17fs. This patient exhibits a complex and severe phenotype including abnormal facial shape, ambiguous genitalia, anteverted nares, cryptorchidism, dysplastic pulmonary valve, hyperbilirubinemia, hypertelorism, intestinal atresia, muscular hypotonia, polyhydramnios, posteriorly rotated ears, premature birth, pulmonary arterial hypertension, pulmonic stenosis, reduced blood folate concentration, a sacral dimple, short nose, being small for gestational age, a thick vermilion border, ventriculomegaly, and a wide intermammary distance.

Patient 37 also carries the homozygous ZNF699 mutation NM_198535.2:c.436_439del (protein p.Asp146Ilefs*10), the same as patients 34 and 35, and is from a consanguineous family. However, this patient's phenotype includes a distinct set of features: abnormal facial shape, microcephaly, hearing impairment, macrotia, a prominent nasal bridge and nose, microphthalmia, abnormal thumb morphology, intellectual disability, and muscular hypotonia.
