### 5. Key Findings
*   **Universal Phenotypes:** All ten patients consistently exhibit growth failure, low birth weight, cachexia/bird-like faces, clinical photosensitivity, and microcephaly.
*   **High Prevalence of Ocular and Neurological Issues:** Cataracts and retinal degeneration (including optic atrophy) are present in eight out of ten patients. Mental retardation is also universally present, with varying degrees of severity (mild, moderate, progressive, severe, or congenital).
*   **Hearing Loss:** Hearing loss is observed in eight patients, being absent only in GM02965 and CCS4.
*   **Absent Phenotypes:** Dental anomalies and arthrogryposis are consistently absent across all patients listed.
*   **Genotype-Phenotype Differences:** Patients GM02965 and CCS4, both classified as "Compound heterozygotes," show a distinct phenotype profile compared to the other eight patients (classified as "I" or "I/II"). Specifically, GM02965 and CCS4 lack microcephaly, cataracts, and hearing loss, and present with "mild" mental retardation, whereas these symptoms are generally present and often more severe in the other patients.
*   **Mutation Diversity:** The table highlights a variety of genetic alterations, including large deletions, inversions, insertions, and point mutations, leading to diverse protein changes.
