Relevant PubMed IDs are 30042658, 29036603, 27339468, and 29881787.

**5. Key Findings:**
*   All five patients detailed in this table segment (Patients 5-9) have identified homozygous variants in the *IPO8* gene.
*   A history of consanguinity is noted for all families, suggesting a potential autosomal recessive inheritance pattern for these *IPO8*-related conditions.
*   *IPO8* gene variations are associated with a highly variable and multi-systemic clinical presentation, encompassing developmental delays, skeletal and connective tissue abnormalities (e.g., Cutis laxa, joint hypermobility, short stature, pectus excavatum), cardiac defects (e.g., VSD, PDA, right ventricular hypertrophy, mitral valve abnormality, pulmonary arterial hypertension), renal issues (hydronephrosis), distinct craniofacial features, and other systemic issues like endocrine dysfunction (hypothyroidism) and feeding difficulties.
*   The evidence supporting *IPO8*'s pathogenicity includes its role in neuronal migration (from mouse models) and its widespread expression as a housekeeping gene, consistent with the diverse phenotypes observed.
*   Patients with identical *IPO8* variants (e.g., Patients 8 and 9 with c.776G>A and p.Trp259*) can exhibit both overlapping and distinct phenotypic features, highlighting the complexity of genotype-phenotype correlation.
