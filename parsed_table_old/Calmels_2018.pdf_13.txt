### Table 4: Summary of clinical features

1.  **Table Title/Caption:** Summary of clinical features
2.  **Table Purpose:** This table provides a comparative summary of the prevalence of various clinical features in patients with Cockayne Syndrome (CS), differentiating between CS-A and CS-B types. It also includes data from two other published studies (Wilson et al. and Kubota et al.) for comparison.
3.  **Table Structure (columns/rows):**
    *   **Columns:** "Clinical feature" (listing specific symptoms), "CS-A*" (prevalence in CS-A patients), "CS-B*" (prevalence in CS-B patients), "Wilson et al†" (prevalence from Wilson et al. study), and "Kubota et al‡" (prevalence from Kubota et al. study).
    *   **Rows:** Each row corresponds to a specific clinical feature, such as Growth failure, Low birth weight, Cachexia, Mental retardation, Microcephaly, Cataracts, Microphthalmia, Retinal degeneration, Deafness, Photosensitivity, Dental anomalies, and CSA/CSB mutations.
4.  **Table Data:**
    The table presents the following prevalence rates for clinical features:
    *   **Growth failure:** Highly prevalent, affecting 28 out of 29 (97%) CS-A patients and 66 out of 67 (99%) CS-B patients. It was 100% in Wilson et al. data and 36 out of 36 (100%) in Kubota et al. data.
    *   **Low birth weight:** Observed in 7 out of 25 (28%) CS-A patients and 24 out of 48 (50%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 0% prevalence.
    *   **Cachexia:** Present in 26 out of 27 (96%) CS-A patients and 55 out of 56 (98%) CS-B patients. Kubota et al. reported 38 out of 39 (97%).
    *   **Mental retardation:** Universally present in CS-A patients (27 out of 27, 100%) and highly prevalent in CS-B patients (55 out of 57, 96%). Kubota et al. also reported 41 out of 41 (100%).
    *   **Microcephaly:** Affects 26 out of 27 (96%) CS-A patients and 55 out of 57 (96%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 100%.
    *   **Cataracts:** Found in 12 out of 22 (54%) CS-A patients and 34 out of 49 (69%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 48%, and Kubota et al. reported 20 out of 31 (65%).
    *   **Microphthalmia:** Less common, seen in 2 out of 16 (12.5%) CS-A patients and 13 out of 33 (39%) CS-B patients.
    *   **Retinal degeneration:** Occurs in 10 out of 18 (55%) CS-A patients and 16 out of 30 (53%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 43%, while Kubota et al. reported a higher prevalence of 25 out of 28 (89%).
    *   **Deafness:** Present in 15 out of 21 (71%) CS-A patients and 26 out of 43 (60%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 44%, and Kubota et al. reported 28 out of 31 (90%).
    *   **Photosensitivity:** Affects 17 out of 23 (74%) CS-A patients and 38 out of 50 (76%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 47%, and Kubota et al. reported 34 out of 37 (92%).
    *   **Dental anomalies:** Observed in 11 out of 14 (78%) CS-A patients and 17 out of 25 (68%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 46%, and Kubota et al. reported 18 out of 25 (72%).
    *   **CSA/CSB mutations:** Identified in all 39 out of 39 (100%) CS-A patients and all 85 out of 85 (100%) CS-B patients. Wilson et al. reported 39 out of 40 (98%).
5.  **Key Findings:**
    *   Core features like growth failure, cachexia, mental retardation, and microcephaly are highly prevalent (often exceeding 90%) in both CS-A and CS-B patients, and these findings are largely consistent across different studies.
    *   Low birth weight is more common in CS-B (50%) than CS-A (28%).
    *   While cataracts, microphthalmia, retinal degeneration, deafness, photosensitivity, and dental anomalies are significant, their prevalence varies, sometimes showing higher rates in CS-B patients or in specific cohorts like Kubota et al.'s (e.g., retinal degeneration and deafness).
    *   The presence of CSA/CSB mutations is nearly universal in patients diagnosed with Cockayne Syndrome across all reported cohorts.

    *   \*Number of cases with indicated feature/total number for whom relevant information is available (% in parentheses).
    *   †Data, expressed as %, from Wilson et al.⁴
    *   ‡Data from Kubota et al.³
    *   §Molecular analysis is only available from 40 families; 39 were mutated in CSA or CSB, and 1 was mutated in XPD.
