**5. Key Findings:**
*   **High Prevalence of Oro-dental Anomalies:** All 14 patients in the cohort exhibited a range of oro-dental anomalies, highlighting their consistent presence in Cockayne Syndrome.
*   **Common Structural Defects:** Microdontia and enamel defects (opacities and hypoplasia) are highly prevalent across both severe and moderate CS types, and in both CSB and CSA gene mutations.
*   **Tooth Agenesis:** Agenesis of multiple teeth was specifically noted in two patients (Patient 7 and Patient 9), indicating a significant developmental anomaly.
*   **Eruption and Crowding Variability:** While some patients had normal tooth eruption, others experienced late or early eruption. Dental crowding was a frequent finding, ranging from moderate to severe.
*   **Periodontal Issues and Oral Hygiene:** Gingivitis was a common periodontal problem, particularly in older patients. Oral hygiene was frequently described as "deficient," likely contributing to the observed caries and periodontal conditions.
*   **Caries Experience:** Caries prevalence varied, with some patients having no caries and others showing significant scores (e.g., Patient 5 with 20/Ø, Patient 11 with 16/4).
*   **Consistent Facial Dysmorphology:** Facial dysmorphology (MB, MB R, MB D) was consistently reported across almost all patients.
*   **Parafunctional Habits:** Bruxism was noted in some of the younger, more severely affected patients.
